Designed and developed an AC-to-DC converter and regulated power supply using Proteus for circuit and PCB design. The system incorporated a transformer, bridge rectifier, filter capacitors, and voltage regulators to provide regulated 5V, 9V, and 12V DC outputs. A potentiometer was used to adjust the DC output voltage, with two dedicated output terminals provided for obtaining variable DC voltage. A DC digital voltmeter was integrated to display the output voltage in real time. The system also included fuses, indicator switches, LEDs for status indication, a 6A circuit breaker for electrical protection, and a 12V DC fan for cooling. The complete circuit was assembled on a custom rectangular PVC board.
